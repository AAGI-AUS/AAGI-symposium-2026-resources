# AI tools for literature review

**AAGI Symposium 2026** · Peiye Li, Curtin University · 12 November 2026

Resources from the talk *AI tools for literature review: finding, extracting and summarising papers, and checking what the tools tell you.*

The tools below are examples, grouped by literature review task. They are not a ranking. Most offer a free tier, and features and pricing change often.

---

## Tools by task

### Discover papers
Find relevant studies and explore a topic.
- [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org): free academic search engine with AI-generated paper summaries
- [Ai2 Asta](https://asta.allen.ai): question-led search that explains why each paper was retrieved
- [Elicit](https://elicit.com): semantic search plus structured extraction
- [Undermind](https://www.undermind.ai): deep, iterative search for a specific research question
- [Consensus](https://consensus.app): cited answers and field overviews from published research

### Screen papers
Apply eligibility criteria to candidate studies.
- [ASReview](https://asreview.nl): open-source, active-learning screening with a human in the loop
- [Elicit](https://elicit.com)

### Read and extract
Turn papers into a structured evidence matrix.
- [Elicit](https://elicit.com): question-driven data matrix, with a supporting passage behind each cell
- [SciSpace](https://scispace.com): extraction tables and chat with PDFs
- [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com): source-linked answers across the papers you upload

### Map the literature
Explore relationships between papers and discover related work.
- [Litmaps](https://www.litmaps.com)
- [ResearchRabbit](https://www.researchrabbit.ai)
- [Connected Papers](https://www.connectedpapers.com)

### Synthesise
Compare findings, methods and open questions.
- [Consensus](https://consensus.app), [SciSpace](https://scispace.com), [Undermind](https://www.undermind.ai)

### Interrogate papers
Ask focused questions about methods and claims.
- [SciSpace](https://scispace.com), [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google.com), [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai)
