# AAGI Symposium 2026 – Workshop Resources

## Overview

This repository contains a collection of materials and resources developed for the **AAGI Symposium 2026**.

The materials have been brought together from different workshop sessions to provide a shared resource for the broader AAGI team, including those who were unable to attend.

## Contents

The resources include:

- **Workshop presentations:** Supporting materials shared by the presenters.
- **Practical examples and demonstrations:** Tools, workflows, and examples introduced during the workshops.
- **Additional resources:** Useful links, references, and learning materials for further exploration.

## Purpose

The aim of this collection is to make the knowledge, tools, and practical insights shared during the symposium accessible to the wider AAGI community.
We encourage everyone to explore the materials, revisit topics of interest, and apply relevant approaches to their own research and workflows.

## Acknowledgements
Many thanks to all workshop presenters, organisers, and contributors for sharing their expertise and preparing these resources.
**These materials are intended for internal knowledge sharing and continued learning across the AAGI teams.**
